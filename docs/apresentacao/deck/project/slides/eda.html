Late payments raise default risk over tenfold

Past late payments and heavy use of revolving credit raise the risk sharply, and older clients default less. Panel (d) shows the flags I created in the EDA: they carry signal of their own.

First, what the training data shows. Past late payments are the strongest signal: the bad rate goes from 4 percent with none to over 40 percent with four or more, more than tenfold. Heavy use of revolving credit raises the risk sharply in the top deciles, and older clients default less. The last panel shows two flags I created: clients without income default slightly less, and clients with codes 96 or 98 default more than half the time.
